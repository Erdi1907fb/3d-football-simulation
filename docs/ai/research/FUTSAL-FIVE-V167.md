The requested 2–1–1 formation, initial contested drop and conceding-forward restart are product rules; this is not presented as a complete official futsal ruleset. Shot style is a seeded contextual decision. Goals and saves remain physical outcomes. Hand control and hand parries require the keeper and ball to be inside the local goal area; outside, ground intervention uses the feet.
